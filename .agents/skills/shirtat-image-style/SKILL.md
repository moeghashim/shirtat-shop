# Shirtat image style

Shirtat images should look like real editorial street-style photography, not flat studio mockups.

## Rules
1. Real-looking outdoor or textured settings (old wooden doors, stone walls, streets) with warm natural light.
2. Model in a relaxed editorial pose; everyday accessories (sunglasses, watch, bracelet) are fine.
3. Oversized fit, slightly faded or washed garment colour, design centred on the chest and fully legible.
4. The Arabic calligraphy must be the exact approved design file. Never redraw, translate or alter it, and never change the Shirtat logo.
5. Square (1:1) for feed posts, vertical (9:16) for reels and stories.
